# The 3-minute demo (open on the NO)

**Setup:** sandbox PayPal credentials + an AI key in env, seeded books
(headroom $1,900, Mortgage/Daycare/Honda committed), phone mirrored.

- **0:00 Cold open, no dashboard.** "This is Cani. You don't check it on
  Sunday — you ask it at the register." Show the headroom and the bars.
- **0:20 Upload a typical month** (Upload tab, sample month). Committed
  candidates appear; tick only what's real. "Inference discovers, human
  confirms, code decides."
- **0:45 Who I am.** Profile tab: the character builds as you tap —
  50s, house paid off, shades on. Take the money blot in two cards.
  "It knows who I am and who I want to be — and it still can't move a number."
- **1:10 The NO.** Ask: Aeron Deck, $2,800. **NO**, in bars, short $900,
  no buy button. Ask again — the shame bypass appears. "The manager makes
  you say it twice, and he keeps score." Don't take it. Yet.
- **1:40 The lane.** Ask insulin at CVS Pharmacy, over headroom: instant
  YES, no shame. Then groceries at Kroger with beer in the cart: the lane
  denies it and names the beer. "Essentials ride free. Treats don't."
- **2:05 Same engine, from an agent.** In Claude: `cani.ask` → NO.
  `checkout.attempt` on the deck → structured decline, *no PayPal order
  exists*. Then a $400 ask → YES → a real sandbox PayPal order is created,
  approve, capture, order id in the ledger.
- **2:40 Take the bypass.** Back in the app, take the shame bypass on the
  deck. The bars roast you, shame +1 lands under the headroom.
  "A budget app shows you charts. Cani is the manager an agent has to ask —
  and even you can't override him for free."
- **2:55 The flip line.** "This isn't a budget. It's a manager."
